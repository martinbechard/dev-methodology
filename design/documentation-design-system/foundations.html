Foundations

Foundations are the low-level values that give unrelated documentation components a related appearance. The source pages share a recognizable palette and system font, but token names and values vary.

Theme Families

The suite uses light pages for reference material and dark pages for narrative lifecycle or compiled-context material.

Light toolkit family

Cool gray page, white panels, dark ink, soft accent fills, and shallow shadows.

index.html and eight light detail pages

Dark narrative family

Dark navy page and panel colors with pale text, stronger borders, and luminous accents.

orchestrated-development-lifecycle.html; wiki-skills-and-project-context.html

Typography

Most pages use an Inter-first system stack. Monospace text marks paths, code, configuration, and exact identifiers.

A lede explains the page’s essence before details.

Body text carries the main explanation. Inline code preserves exact identifiers.

The newer evaluation and lifecycle pages use larger display titles and tighter letter spacing. The original toolkit family uses smaller titles and neutral tracking.

Radii And Shadows

Rectangular content uses modest rounding. Navigation pills and status labels use a full pill radius.

Controls, logos, and compact labels.
